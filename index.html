Gymothy privacy & app policy

Policy 1.4 · Last reviewed 28 October 2026

Gymothy helps you record your training and wellbeing. This policy explains the current Android, iOS, and web app, how your information is handled, and the limits of its fitness and nutrition features.

Privacy and support: ninojacobs1@gmail.com

At a glance

Your profile and gym records are stored on your device or in this browser. There is no Gymothy account, developer-operated storage server, or automatic sync between devices.

Online motivation connects to DummyJSON. Exporting a backup creates a readable file containing your personal records. Device backups and any hosting or sharing service you choose have their own privacy practices.

Nutrition targets and progress calculations are estimates for general fitness. Gymothy is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

Information you choose to enter

Gymothy can store your name and training focus; date of birth and calculated age; height and weigh-ins; nutrition equation setting, activity level, and weight goal; gym visit dates and times; exercises, individual lifting sets, reps and weights; cardio time, distance and steps; splits and templates; food and macro totals, water and custom nutrition goals; sleep, energy and wellbeing notes; and unit and appearance preferences.

The app also stores setup status, catalogue versions, and the daily motivation cache so it can remember your choices and display your records. Free-text fields can contain whatever you enter; avoid adding information you do not need for your training.

Profile setup can be skipped. Measurements and health details are optional, although some calculations need particular inputs. Steps, distance, exercise performance and food intake are entered manually. Gymothy does not currently read your GPS, contacts, HealthKit, Health Connect, or a wearable device.

How the app uses your information

Your entries are used locally to personalise your greeting, show your current split, record workouts and attendance, calculate charts, comparisons, personal bests and milestones, and estimate nutrition goals. Every workout save, including cardio and edits from any logging screen, records a gym visit for the workout date. An existing visit on that date is reused. There is no workout-only option, so a saved activity is counted as attendance even if you performed it outside a gym.

New strength exercises start with one blank set; you add the sets you actually complete. Editing a saved workout retains its recorded sets. Collapsing or expanding an exercise card changes its display and is retained in the local draft; it does not complete or log the workout. Save workout records the full session and attendance together.

Workout edits are automatically cached on this device as unfinished drafts, including incomplete inputs, notes, set details, gym-visit time, display units and collapsed cards. There is one draft per selected date, with separate drafts for different dates. Reopening the logger for a date resumes its draft; Workouts also lists drafts with Continue and Discard actions. Changing the selected date keeps the previous date’s draft. Drafts do not count toward attendance, progress or completed training until Save workout succeeds. Saving clears that date’s draft; a failed save retains it. A draft saved in different measurement units keeps those units when resumed.

Age is calculated from your date of birth. Profile weight follows your latest recorded weigh-in. Automatic nutrition estimates use your logged weight and nutrition profile; manual day, week and everyday overrides are kept until you edit or clear them.

Gymothy has no advertising, advertising tracking, developer-operated usage analytics, or sale of your records in this version. Your profile and training records are not uploaded to the developer as part of ordinary app use.

Local storage and security

Android and iOS save records in the app’s local storage. Web records are saved in browser storage for that website address; another browser, website address or device has separate records. A web host receives ordinary connection information when it serves the app, such as an IP address and browser request details. Its logs are separate from your locally stored gym records.

Gymothy does not add its own encryption, password lock or biometric lock to saved records. Device and browser protections still apply, but local storage is not a guarantee against access on an unlocked, compromised or shared device. Use a device lock, keep software updated, and protect backup files.

When upgrading an earlier Android installation, Gymothy can import its previous local records on the same device. The older local copy is retained to avoid losing your records during that import; this is not an upload or sync service.

Internet connections and third parties

Daily motivation automatically requests a quote from DummyJSON when the app is opened, returns to the foreground, or checks for a new day, subject to its cache and retry limits. Refresh daily motivation makes another request. Only Home displays the quote. The request does not include your name, date of birth, health records or workout entries, but DummyJSON and its hosting providers can receive your IP address and ordinary request information, and may process it outside your country.

Quotes are cached locally for the day, with local fallback messages if the request fails. Disconnecting from the internet prevents new online requests; there is currently no separate in-app switch for online motivation. Quote attribution is supplied by the provider and is not independently verified.

Opening an external website or your email app contacts that provider. Exporting through a share sheet may give the file to an app, person or storage service you select. Their own policies apply to information they receive. Gymothy does not control their retention or security.

Backups, sharing and restoring

Settings → Data → Export backup creates an unencrypted JSON file containing your profile, health information, gym history, unfinished workout drafts, splits, settings and other saved app records. Anyone with the file can read its contents. On mobile, an export or import may also leave a temporary copy in the app’s cache. Save and share backups only with people and services you trust.

Import backup replaces this installation’s records after validation and your confirmation. A backup can restore information that you previously deleted. Gymothy cannot retrieve a lost backup or remove copies from someone else’s device, email, cloud account or storage service.

Android currently allows operating-system backup and device transfer. Depending on your device settings, records may be copied by those services even if you do not export a file yourself. The iOS storage library currently excludes its saved app records from iCloud backup by default. These are separate from a backup you choose to export, and from any browser or device-management tools you use.

Retention, correction and deletion

Records and unfinished workout drafts have no automatic expiry and normally remain in this installation until you edit or remove them or clear its storage. You can edit your profile and entries in the app and export a copy under Settings → Data.

In Gym log, select a calendar date to edit or remove its individual visits. Editing a visit date or time changes attendance only; removing a visit keeps workout history and progress. Saving a workout for that date again records attendance if no visit remains.

Clear selected data can reset attendance, workout progress, splits, templates, weight history, nutrition logs, custom goals, wellbeing or the exercise library. Clearing workout history and progress also clears unfinished workout drafts. Resetting the exercise library clears both workout history and drafts to keep references consistent. Discard draft removes the selected date’s unfinished editor without deleting completed workouts; copies in exported files or device backups remain separate. These resets deliberately keep your name, profile, date of birth, height, nutrition profile, units and theme. There is currently no in-app action that erases every category including your profile.

To remove all local records on Android, use the device’s Clear storage / Clear app data controls for Gymothy. On iOS, delete the app rather than offloading it. On web, clear that website’s browser storage. Also remove any exported files, temporary copies and system backups that you want deleted. Ordinary app deletion is not a promise of secure erasure from every backup or storage medium.

For information handled by the maintainer, you may request access, correction or deletion, or raise an objection or withdraw consent where applicable, using the contact below. The maintainer cannot remotely inspect, change or erase records that exist only on your device. Describe your request without attaching health records or a full backup unless a suitable private exchange has been agreed.

Fitness, nutrition and health limitations

Gymothy provides general fitness tracking and estimates, not medical care, diagnosis or an individual treatment plan. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and a suitably qualified professional for personal training or nutrition needs. Templates, suggested goals and motivational quotes are not a professional assessment of your circumstances.

Automatic nutrition estimates are supported for adults aged 18–100 and use the latest applicable weigh-in, age, height, equation setting, activity level and selected goal. Calorie estimates use the Mifflin–St Jeor equation with an activity factor and a goal adjustment; protein and other macro targets use general calculation rules. They do not account for every condition, medication, pregnancy, breastfeeding, injury, eating disorder or individual nutritional need. Manual overrides are your own targets, not a safety check or professional approval.

Charts and personal bests reflect the records you enter and may contain errors or omissions. A weight comparison can use the closest available weigh-in and shows the date actually used. Cardio distance, steps and time are manually logged, not sensor measurements. Templates and past progress do not guarantee results or that a particular exercise, load or target is safe for you.

Children and younger users

Gymothy is intended for adult fitness tracking and does not offer a children’s service or a parental-consent system. Automatic nutrition estimates are limited to ages 18–100. A date of birth alone does not verify age or parental permission. Do not submit a child’s records to the maintainer or share them through a support request.

Trainer access and future features

This version does not provide trainer accounts, client accounts, a shared client dashboard, automatic trainer access or cloud sync. Someone who receives a full backup can read the personal and health information in it; a backup is not a limited invitation to view only a split.

If trainer/client sharing, accounts, cloud storage, advertising, analytics, AI services or new health integrations are introduced, this policy must be updated to explain the actual data flows and controls. Permissions and consent must be requested where required before new collection or sharing begins; reading this page is not blanket consent to future uses.

Use of the app and content

Use Gymothy for records you are entitled to enter or share. Keep your entries accurate, protect your device and retain any backups you need. Availability, error-free calculations, recovery of lost data and fitness results are not guaranteed. Nothing in this policy excludes rights or remedies that applicable law does not allow to be excluded.

Exercise names, third-party quotes and linked materials may belong to their respective owners. Linking to a service or showing an attribution does not imply its endorsement or give permission to reuse its material elsewhere. Online content and external services can change or become unavailable.

Contact and privacy requests

Gymothy is maintained by Nino Jacobs. For privacy questions, support, corrections to this policy or concerns about information handled by the maintainer, contact ninojacobs1@gmail.com. Contacting us sends your email address and message to the maintainer and email provider so we can respond to your request or investigate the issue. This correspondence is separate from local app storage and can remain in email records and provider backups until deleted under their retention settings. Clearing Gymothy does not delete email correspondence. Do not send passwords, medical documents or a full Gymothy backup in an initial message.

Where South African privacy law applies, you can also raise a privacy complaint with the Information Regulator. Depending on your location and the processing involved, other privacy rights and authorities may apply.
